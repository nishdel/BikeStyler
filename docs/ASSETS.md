## Generated product references

The [machine-readable manifest](../public/components/photographic/asset-manifest.json) retains source URLs, attachment geometry and limitations for:

- Velo Orange Enterprise front wheel: silver rim, hub and spokes; reused as an approximate rear-wheel appearance.
- Brooks B17 Racing Green: leather saddle and rails; variants share the approximate green image.
- ENE Ciclo double crank: polished chainrings and foreshortened crank arm; original pedal is protected separately.

Generation sought transparent product-shaped cutouts with restrained warm highlights and side-view geometry. Static image preparation occurred during development; no model is called by the application. Exact colours, lettering, dimensions, spoke/tooth counts and mechanical details are not certified. No manufacturer endorsement is implied. Original reference photographs, private generation context and source-room photographs are not redistributed.

Component lighting controls affect displayed RGB while preserving alpha. Photographic compositing still needs manual alignment and foreground protection. Hidden areas behind the original crank remain unknown.
